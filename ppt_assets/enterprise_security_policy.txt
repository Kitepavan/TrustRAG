ENTERPRISE IT SECURITY POLICY — 2026
Document Owner: IT Security Office
Classification: INTERNAL
Version: 2.4 (Approved)

1. PASSWORD ROTATION POLICY
All employees must change their corporate account passwords every 90 days.
Passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters and include uppercase, lowercase,
numeric, and symbol characters. Reuse of the previous five passwords is prohibited.

2. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all employees accessing
corporate email, the VPN, and any internal business application. MFA must be
enabled at account provisioning and may not be disabled without written
approval from the IT Security Office.

3. ENCRYPTION AT REST AND IN TRANSIT
All stored confidential data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256. All
network traffic between corporate systems must use TLS 1.2 or higher.

4. INCIDENT REPORTING
Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@company.example
within 60 minutes of discovery. Employees must never attempt to investigate
or remediate an incident on their own.

5. ACCEPTABLE USE
Corporate devices are for business use only. Installation of unapproved
software is prohibited and will be treated as a security violation.
